Privacy notice

This shop is run by [BUSINESS NAME] in Nakuru, Kenya. This page explains what we do with your information when you place an order. You must be 18 or older to order.

What we collect

  • Phone number, to send the payment prompt, text you about your order, and let the rider reach you.
  • Delivery spot: the map pin and/or landmark you give us.
  • Your order: items, quantity, amount, payment method and payment receipt reference.
  • Your internet address (IP), kept with the order only to stop abuse such as order spam.

We never see your M-Pesa PIN or Airtel Money PIN. You enter it on your own phone.

Why, and who sees it

  • To deliver your order. The rider assigned to your order sees your phone number and delivery spot, only for that delivery.
  • Payments. Safaricom (M-Pesa) or Airtel (Airtel Money) process your payment under their own terms.
  • Text messages. Our SMS provider, Africa's Talking, delivers the texts we send you.
  • Hosting. The site runs on Cloudflare.
  • Maps. The map on the order form loads from OpenStreetMap, which can see that your device asked for map tiles. If you tap "Use my current location", your phone asks your permission first, and we use the location only for that order.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 12 months] for accounting, refunds and disputes, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it (where we are not required to keep it), and object to how we use it. If we say no, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
